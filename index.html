About

I'm a creative technologist and researcher. I start from how people live with technology, and turn that into interfaces, data visualisations and working prototypes.

My background is in cultural anthropology and digital humanities, so research comes first: interviews, usability tests, fieldwork and data analysis. From there I design and build, in Figma and in code.

I've done practice-based research at the Institute of Network Cultures and mapped digital cultural spaces with The Hmm and DEN. My projects deal with museum collections, archives, public data and online therapy, and I care that the result is inclusive, well evidenced and easy to use.

ResearchInterviews · usability testing (SUS, think-aloud) · ethnography · personas · data analysis
DesignInformation architecture · interaction and visual design · prototyping in Figma
BuildReact · D3.js · Leaflet · Python · RDF, SPARQL and OWL · Hydra
