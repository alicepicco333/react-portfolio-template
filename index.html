02 — AboutBased in Amsterdam

I work at the intersection of culture, technology & design.

I'm Alice, an anthropologist and digital humanities expert based in Amsterdam, blending critical research with creative technologies across cultural analysis, web development, and interactive design.

Weaving together critical research and creative experimentation. With a background in cultural anthropology and a strong foundation in digital humanities, my projects range from interactive archives and feminist data visualizations to live-coded performances and augmented reality design. Whether I’m mapping digital territories, building ontologies of cognitive bias, or glitching visual narratives in real time, I’m driven by curiosity and a commitment to inclusive, ethical tech. I enjoy working across media and disciplines — from semantic web development to storytelling with code — always with a reflexive, research-oriented approach.
