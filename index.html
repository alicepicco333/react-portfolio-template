    About

    I'm an HCI researcher and designer. I start from how people actually use technology, test it with them, and turn what I learn into clear, inclusive interfaces, data visualisations and working prototypes.

    My background is in cultural anthropology and digital humanities, so research comes first: interviews, usability tests, fieldwork and data analysis. From there I design and build, in Figma and in code.

    I've worked as a UX researcher and designer at BrainBite, an AI tutoring platform for children, on support for neurodiverse learners; done practice-based research as a visiting research fellow at the Institute of Network Cultures; and taken part in The Hmm's Voices of Makers programme with DEN. My projects deal with museum collections, archives, public data and online therapy, and I care that the result is inclusive, well evidenced and easy to use.

    DesignInformation architecture · interaction and visual design · prototyping in Figma
