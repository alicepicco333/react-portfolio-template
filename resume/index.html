Anthropology / Digital Humanities / Design

I work at the intersection of culture, technology, and design, weaving together critical research and creative experimentation. With a background in cultural anthropology and a strong foundation in digital humanities, my projects range from interactive archives and feminist data visualizations to live-coded performances and augmented reality design. Whether I’m mapping digital territories, building ontologies of cognitive bias, or glitching visual narratives in real time, I’m driven by curiosity and a commitment to inclusive, ethical tech. I enjoy working across media and disciplines — from semantic web development to storytelling with code — always with a reflexive, research-oriented approach.
