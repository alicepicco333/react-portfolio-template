The Excavation Suit

The Excavation Suit is a speculative wearable for a Multimodal Interaction Design course: a suit for the descendants of a colony on Proxima Centauri b, who reopen the nuclear waste sites their ancestors sealed.

Brief
Colonists on Proxima Centauri b reopen buried nuclear waste, and radio fails under the star's flares.
Role
Concept and interaction design (course project).
Method
Every hazard signalled across touch, vision and sound; gesture as the primary input.
Outcome
In progress: an Arduino glove and helmet rig for the midterm.
Stack
Arduino · sensors · OLED
Date
Sep 2026
Context
Multimodal Interaction Design course
Category
Work in Progress
Techniques
Speculative Design

Notes

Radio fails under the star's flares, so gesture is a primary input, and every hazard registers across at least two of touch, vision and sound. This follows the Sandia recommendation that nuclear warnings be layered across several channels, and the "ray cat" idea of a warning that needs no literacy. Before leaving, the wearer marks the site for the next crew.

The midterm prototype is an Arduino glove and forearm rig for the haptic and gesture loops, with a helmet-mounted colour sensor and an OLED display.
