Words of Gender

Words of Gender is the prototype of an archive of women authors. Taking its lead from Vera Gheno's Parole d'altro genere (Rizzoli, 2023), it asks how different authors, in different historical and cultural contexts, have written about the same concepts tied to gender: beauty, grace, strength, independence, marriage, maternity.

Notes

Excerpts from nine women authors spanning five centuries are encoded in TEI XML, linked to open data and marked up for the concepts they touch, so the concepts can be followed across texts as a network of variations and cross-references rather than read one author at a time.

The project starts from the gap in how women authors are represented in the canon and in course readings, and is designed as an archive meant to keep growing.
