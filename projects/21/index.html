Words of Gender

Words of Gender is the prototype of an archive of women authors. Taking its lead from Vera Gheno's Parole d'altro genere (Rizzoli, 2023), it asks how different authors, in different historical and cultural contexts, have written about the same concepts tied to gender: beauty, grace, strength, independence, marriage, maternity.

Words of Gender began as a group project with Nicole Liggeri, Lucia Bertoldini and Chiara Parravicini for Digital Text in the Humanities. I reworked it independently in 2026; the version shown here is that rework.

Visit project ↗

Notes

Excerpts from nine women authors spanning five centuries (1558 to 1958), in Italian, French and English, are encoded in TEI XML and linked to VIAF, Wikidata and WorldCat. 172 passages are tagged with 27 concepts, so each concept can be followed across texts as a network of variations and cross-references rather than read one author at a time.

The 2026 version turns the prototype into a romantic reading edition in brown and pink: Pinyon Script for the masthead and author names, Cormorant Garamond for the passages, fleurons and framed letters; a reading view where up to four concepts are highlighted at once, never by colour alone; an author-by-concept matrix that opens every passage on a concept side by side; and a timeline of first editions.
