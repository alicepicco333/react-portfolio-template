OULAD Critical Dataset Analysis

A living critical notebook for HCI Theory and Data that follows one dataset for eight weeks: the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD).

Date
Sep 2026
Category
Work in Progress
Techniques
Critical Data Studies, HCI

Notes

Each week adds a short entry and nothing is deleted: superseded decisions stay visible, with a change log and a log of AI use. It begins with a positionality note and asks what the dataset's categories capture and leave out about the students in it.
