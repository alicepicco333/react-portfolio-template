Digitalian Folktales

Digitalian Folktales is a digital edition of tales from Italo Calvino's Fiabe italiane, read alongside the regional versions Calvino drew on.

Date
2023 – 2024
Category
Research
Techniques
Digital Edition
Visit project ↗

Notes

The comparison view sets two versions of the same tale side by side, such as Gesù e San Pietro in Friuli and in Sicilia, each with its original dialect text and an Italian translation. For every tale the edition records where it comes from, its narrators, Calvino's source collection, its ATU tale type and a Propp-based reading of its outcome, animals and places, and an advanced search runs across the corpus.
