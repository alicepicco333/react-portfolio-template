The Stolen Archive

The Stolen Archive is a virtual exhibition of thirty objects taken during colonial wars, conquests and excavations, from the Benin Bronzes to the Parthenon Marbles. It works as a companion to a museum visit and as a museum of its own, and asks who was able to take these objects, who keeps them and who has managed to get them back.

Notes

Visitors pick one of three tours (Timeline, Colonial Conquests, Geo) and choose how long and how complex the texts should be: every object has nine versions of its text for each tour, 810 in all. Each object page explains how the object left, who claims it and who holds it, with the sources for each position. An atlas traces the thirty routes on a map, and a floor plan places the objects in thirteen rooms over two floors.

Developed for Information Modelling and Web Technologies (prof. Fabio Vitali) in the Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge master's at the University of Bologna. Built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with inline SVG maps, 3D views of the objects, an RDF graph for every record, and dark and light themes.
