The Meme Ontology

MEMO is an ontology of internet memes, from my master's research Towards an Ontology of Memes (supervisor Fabio Vitali), which I began as a research intern at the Institute of Network Cultures in Amsterdam.

Date
2024 – 2026
Context

With
Solo project
Category
Research
Techniques
Ontology Engineering, Semantic Web

Notes

Following Lev Manovich's cultural analytics, it treats memes as a large dataset rather than isolated texts. Around 5,000 images and their metadata, scraped from Know Your Meme, are described in an OWL ontology informed by FRBR across three areas: visual content, cultural references and format. CLIP is used to classify them semi-automatically.

The ontology is published with LODE documentation, interactive visualisations of formats, platforms, periods and variants, and a SPARQL explorer with example queries, including links to Wikidata for regions and platforms.
