Teigetje & Woelrat

A collection website for Teigetje & Woelrat, an Amsterdam label working across fashion, design and interior art. I designed it in Figma and built it as a React site.

Date
2025 – 2026
Category
Design
Techniques
UX Design, Web Development

Notes

The site has a collection page of ready-to-wear pieces with filters, an archive of pieces from past collections, a history of the label told as a timeline from 1972 onwards, an about page and a contact page, all in English and Dutch.

Built with React, TypeScript and Vite from the Figma design.
