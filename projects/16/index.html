Mary Shelley HTR

A handwritten text recognition campaign on Mary Shelley's manuscripts: we trained Transkribus models to transcribe her handwriting automatically, to help the Shelley-Godwin Archive add new material faster.

Notes

The training set was 64 pages written only by Shelley, 36 from chapters 5 to 7 of Frankenstein and 28 from Mathilda, with the archive's transcriptions as ground truth and Transkribus' Universal Lines model for the layout. We trained two models, one fine-tuned from the English M4 model and one from scratch, with a batch size of 10 over 100 epochs.

On an unseen page of Mathilda the fine-tuned model was clearly more accurate. The ruler visible in the scans was read as text, which cropping would fix, and a three-day queue on Transkribus' free tier left no time to recalibrate. Final project for Semantic Digital Libraries (University of Bologna, 2024) with Gaia Ortona and Chiara Parravicini. I worked on model training and built the website.
