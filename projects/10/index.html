Collaboratory Creative Coding Jam

A Hydra Video Synth workshop I proposed and led in the HDSC2024 open programme of Hackers & Designers, at Het Wilde Weg in Amsterdam on 20 July 2024.

Category
Live Coding
Techniques
Workshop Facilitation, Live Coding
Visit project ↗

Notes

Hydra is an open-source, browser-based live coding tool by Olivia Jack, inspired by modular synths. After an introduction to its primitives, textures and commands, up to fifteen participants made visuals from scratch, remixed video and live streams, and closed the afternoon with a collective live jam.
