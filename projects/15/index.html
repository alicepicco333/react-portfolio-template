Culture4All

Culture for All maps access to culture across Italy: where libraries, archives and museums are, how people use libraries, and how reading habits differ from region to region.

Category
Past Projects
Techniques
Data Visualization, Web Development
Visit project ↗

Notes

Built on open data from ISTAT, dati.cultura and cultural institutions, the site has five interactive visualisations: a choropleth map of libraries by province, a pie chart of library holdings, a pin map of libraries, archives and museums, library loans grouped by region, area, population or urban classification, and reading habits by region.

Institutions cluster in Lombardy, Veneto, Tuscany and Lazio, cities borrow more than rural areas, and reading habits vary widely, which points to where cultural infrastructure needs investment. Made for Information Visualization (2023/24, University of Bologna) with Corrado Consiglio and Salvatore Di Marzo, who gathered and cleaned the data. I designed the visualisations and built the site with Leaflet, D3.js and Chart.js.
