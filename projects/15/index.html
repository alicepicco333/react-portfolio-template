Culture4All

Culture for All maps access to culture across Italy: where libraries, archives and museums are, how people use libraries, and how reading habits differ from region to region.

Date
2024 · reworked 2026
Context
Information Visualization, 2023/24, MA Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge, University of Bologna
With

Category
Research
Techniques
Data Visualization, Web Development

Culture for All began as a group project with Corrado Consiglio and Salvatore Di Marzo for Information Visualization (2023/24). I reworked it independently in 2026; the version shown here is that rework.

Visit project ↗

Notes

Built on open data from ISTAT and the Ministry of Culture, the 2026 version is a data story on unequal access to culture: headline figures, a per-capita map from ISTAT's own rate of libraries per 10,000 inhabitants next to the raw counts, ranked bars instead of a pie chart, loans per library, reading habits by region and a loans-versus-reading comparison, with the finding stated and a data table under every chart.

Institutions cluster in Lombardy, Veneto, Tuscany and Lazio, cities borrow more than rural areas, and reading habits vary widely, which points to where cultural infrastructure needs investment. Made for Information Visualization (2023/24, University of Bologna) with Corrado Consiglio and Salvatore Di Marzo, who gathered and cleaned the data. I designed the visualisations and built the site with Leaflet, D3.js and Chart.js.
