An ontology for a cluster of cognitive biases, part of a collective ontology of human biases built by the whole class of Knowledge Representation and Extraction (prof. Aldo Gangemi, University of Bologna, 2022/23).

The ontologies were built as a group project with Marco Lamorte, Corrado Consiglio and Salvatore Di Marzo for Knowledge Representation and Extraction (2022/23). In 2026 I independently built a visual explorer from the group's OWL files and documentation; the images here come from it.

Notes

Our group, Marco Lamorte, Corrado Consiglio, Salvatore Di Marzo and I, modelled sixteen biases, from the gambler's fallacy and the hot hand fallacy to pareidolia, naive realism and the blind spot bias. Each went through the same eXtreme Design workflow: research and documentation, ideation and design, modelling and implementation, then presentation and visualisation. ChatGPT and Gemini were used early on for definitions, scenarios and user stories, and Framester among the resources for modelling.

The process is documented in a public GitBook. It showed how much understanding of a domain an ontology needs before any modelling starts, and where language models help and where their answers had to be corrected. The 2026 explorer draws each user story as a graph of the individuals in its OWL file and shows where each class comes from (the team's own model, Framester or a design pattern); maps which design patterns and Framester frames each ontology reuses; and shows the vocabulary the group shared.
