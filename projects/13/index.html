Archivio Fondazione Luki Massa

Digitisation of the archive of Luki Massa, which documents lesbian and feminist activism from 1976 to 2015 and was inventoried by Emanuela Fiorletta in 2023.

Brief
Digitising the archive of Luki Massa, which documents lesbian and feminist activism from 1976 to 2015.
Role
Digitisation and cataloguing.
Method
A shared file nomenclature (archive, series, file, unit, page) and a catalogue record for every unit.
Outcome
In progress: five series being digitised.
Stack
Scanning · spreadsheets
Date
2026 –
Context
Archivio Luki Massa, Bologna
Category
Work in Progress
Techniques
Digital Archives, Digitisation

Notes

The work covers five of the archive's series: political groups, lesbian and feminist conferences, seminars and demonstrations, other lesbian, feminist and LGBTQI+ groups, the lesbian and feminist press, and Luki Massa's own notes and writings. Every scan follows a shared nomenclature (archive, series, subseries, file, documentary unit, page), and spreadsheets record the title, date, medium, material and a short description of each unit.
