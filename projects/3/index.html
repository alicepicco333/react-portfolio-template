wipEU

wipEU is an open data project on how the representation of women in positions of power and decision-making correlates across the European Union, and how it has changed over time.

Date
Feb 2024
Category
Research
Techniques
Data Visualization, Digital Ethics
Visit project ↗

Notes

We combined EIGE datasets across six areas (public administration, business and finance, politics, environment and climate change, education and research, law and institutions) into new mashup datasets, and assessed their quality, legal and ethical aspects as open data. In 2023, for example, women made up 20% of the highest decision-making bodies of European financial institutions and led 26.8% of major political parties. In 2026 I redesigned the site so the data leads: six small multiples against a parity line, a chart of change since 2017, a choropleth of shares by country with a year slider, and an explorer for the European institutions, each readable by keyboard and as a table.

Final project for Open Access and Digital Ethics (2023/24, University of Bologna) with Salvatore Di Marzo and Matteo Guenci. I worked on the idea, data retrieval, the visualisations, the quality and legal analyses and the website, built with Leaflet and Plotly.js. The mashup datasets are released under CC BY 4.0.
